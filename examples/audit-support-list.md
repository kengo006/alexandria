### The ❌, in full

> Draft sentence: "Even Roe concedes that the wanting is itself formed by earlier practice (1998, p. 12)."

Page 12 reads: *"Nothing is raised in a public square that did not already have people willing to raise it. The stone is downstream of the wanting."* — Roe says the wanting comes **first** and is the cause. She does not concede the draft's point; she denies it.

⇒ **This is the load-bearing sentence of the section's reply to its strongest objection**, and it rests on a reading the page does not carry. **Nothing ❌ is quietly left in**: the sentence goes back to the Writer, and the reply to Roe has to be built some other way — or conceded.

📌 A search of the rest of Roe (1998) for a passage that *would* support the draft's reading returned nothing; that is a negative result with its denominator: chapters 1–3 and the conclusion, searched for `precede / prior / formed by / practice`, in the text layer and then confirmed on two rendered pages.
